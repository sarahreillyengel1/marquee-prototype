# Marquee Profile Architecture (v2)

**Canonical spec. This is the source of truth for the profile structure. Supersedes the prototype's layout.**

## Core principle
Marquee is not a resume builder, website builder, or LinkedIn clone. It is a **modular professional storytelling platform.** Every profile uses the same design system, but different professions surface different content through editorial layouts and customization.

Users can: edit content anytime · choose Highlights · feature content within modules · hide/show sections · choose approved layouts · reorder sections (within the shared design system).

## Hero
Always: Photo · Name · Headline · Location · Primary CTA.
**Work With Me** lives in the Hero as an **expandable module** (an engagement layer, not profile content). Options: Office Hours · Fractional Role · Project Work · Speaking · Advisory Board · Coaching · Content Partnerships.

## Highlights
Immediately below the Hero. The editorial "cover" of the profile. Up to **3**. **Cross-profile** — can only come from content that already exists elsewhere (Experience, a Project within Experience, Media, Impact, Testimonial, Award, Case Study). Distinct from "Featured" (which is within-module).

## Experience  *(primary section, on homepage; multi-layered)*
Experience Overview · Current Focus · Timeline · Projects attached to an Experience · Featured Experiences (within the module) · View Full Experience → dedicated Experience page.
Projects belong to Experiences (e.g. Product Launch, Campaign, Fundraising, Acquisition, Redesign, Product Development).

## Leadership
Always visible: MBTI · Enneagram · Archetype.
Conditional — "Have you led a team?" If yes: Years Leading · Largest Team · Leadership Philosophy.

## Impact
No changes. Keep the existing Impact system.

## Testimonials
Dedicated module. Expandable.

## Superpowers
Top 3 Superpowers.

## Skills
Skills · Industries · Expertise.

## Values
Working values.

## Education
Education · Certifications.

## Media
On profile: 4 featured items → View All → dedicated Media page.
Categories: Articles · Podcasts · Awards · Videos · Press · Portfolio · Case Studies · **Resources** (replaces Downloads).

## Bio
Short preview on profile → Read Full Bio → dedicated Bio page.

## Store  *(optional)*
Dedicated page. Only appears if products or digital offerings exist.

## Dedicated pages
Homepage surfaces summaries; some modules expand into dedicated pages for depth: Bio · Experience · Media · Store (if applicable).

## Module types
- **Standard** — display directly on the profile: Skills · Values · Superpowers.
- **Expandable** — summary first, then expand inline: Work With Me · Leadership · Impact · Testimonials.
- **Summary + Dedicated Page** — curated preview on profile, full content on its own page: Experience · Media · Bio · Store.

## Editorial system — two levels of featuring
1. **Highlights** (cross-profile) — the 3 strongest pieces of proof across the whole profile, surfaced beneath the Hero.
2. **Featured within modules** — the most important content within a specific module (Featured Experiences, Featured Articles/Videos, Featured Testimonials).

Highlights = the 3 most important stories across the entire profile. Featured = the most important content within a specific module.
