How the header adapts

  • Name sizes itself by length — 56 / 48 / 40px — and never runs past two lines.
  • Currently (serif, labelled) sizes itself too, so all 160 characters always fit. Nothing is cut off.
  • Your short lines (sans, under your title) keep your line breaks and show up to three lines.
  • Previous — brand names as text, up to eight, inside the header with no rules around it. Hidden when empty.
  • Location sits at the bottom, beside your social links.
  • Known for shows your first four, then “+N more.” You choose which four in the builder.
  • Open to shows up to three in the card; everything you offer is on Work with Me.
  • Actions are not part of the header. They are the first section below it: one to four tiles that stretch to fill the row.
  • Edges are 0px everywhere: card, chips, buttons, tiles, photo.
  • Verified appears only after an ID check. Off by default.
  • No photo yet? The card holds its shape with a quiet placeholder (see Length test).
  • Classic — black and white, Stone buttons, Powder tiles, and a light touch of Wine on the title line and action labels.
  • Warm — Michaela's live page today: stone paper, sage accent, soft-blue button, Peach / Powder / Stone tints.
  • Mono — monochromatic in Stone, light to dark. One version, no colour choice.
  • Bold — black, white, Lavender and Wine, in large blocks.
  • Type — Canela for display, Poppins for labels and short headlines, Inter for body. From your Design Language sheet.
  • Looks change colour only. Layout, type and spacing are identical in all four.
